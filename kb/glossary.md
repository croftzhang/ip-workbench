> The desk is illustrative. It does not describe any institution.

## 1. Accounting terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| AFS | Available for sale: a security carried at fair value, with unrealized gains and losses in AOCI |
| HTM | Held to maturity: a security carried at amortised cost; changes in its market value do not reach equity |
| AOCI | Accumulated other comprehensive income: the part of equity that holds unrealized gains and losses on AFS securities |
| Book value | Amortised cost: what was paid, adjusted for the premium or discount recognised so far |
| Mark | The end-of-day price assigned to a position |
| Market value | Current face multiplied by the mark |
| Unrealized gain or loss | Market value less book value |
| Effective yield | The single yield at which a security's expected cash flows, prepayments included, discount to its book value; interest income is recognised at this yield |
| Premium amortisation | The part of a price paid above par that is charged against income over the life of the security |

## 2. Rate-risk measures

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Basis point (bp) | One hundredth of one percent |
| Duration | Percentage change in value for a one percentage point change in rates. Effective duration is measured with prepayments projected again after the shock |
| Net duration | Duration of the securities after the swaps that hedge them |
| Convexity | How much duration changes as rates change. Negative convexity means a security shortens when rates fall and lengthens when they rise |
| DV01 | Dollar change in value for a one basis point change in rates |
| Key-rate exposure | The part of the rate exposure that belongs to one maturity point on the curve; the key-rate exposures add up to the total |
| Duration drift | The change in net duration caused by a rate shock |
| Economic value | The market value of all securities and hedges, AFS and HTM together |
| NII | Net interest income: interest income and reinvestment income, plus hedge carry, less funding cost |
| Earnings at risk | The change in twelve-month NII under a rate shock |
| WAL | Weighted-average life: the average time until principal is returned, in years |

## 3. Mortgage terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Pass-through | A mortgage security that passes borrowers' principal and interest to investors |
| WAC, WALA, WAM | A pool's average loan rate, average loan age and average remaining term |
| CPR | Conditional prepayment rate: the annualised share of a pool's balance that is prepaid |
| SMM | Single monthly mortality: the share of a pool's balance prepaid in one month |
| Incentive | The pool's WAC less the current mortgage rate, in percentage points; positive when refinancing would save the borrower money |
| S-curve | The shape of the refinancing response to the incentive: flat, then steep, then flat again |
| Turnover | Prepayments that come from home sales and not from refinancing |
| Lock-in | Borrowers with loan rates far below the market move home less often |
| Burnout | The weaker refinancing response of a pool that has already lost its most rate-sensitive borrowers |
| Contraction | The shortening of a mortgage security when rates fall and prepayments speed up |
| Extension | The lengthening of a mortgage security when rates rise and prepayments slow |
| Mortgage basis | The gap between the primary mortgage rate and the 10-year Treasury yield |
| Spread | The extra yield over the Treasury curve at which a security is valued |

## 4. Hedging terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Swap | A contract to exchange fixed-rate for floating-rate interest payments on a notional amount |
| Pay-fixed swap | The desk pays the fixed rate and receives the floating rate; it gains when rates rise and shortens net duration |
| Receive-fixed swap | The desk receives the fixed rate and pays the floating rate; it gains when rates fall and lengthens net duration |
| Par swap | A swap whose fixed rate is set so that it has no value at inception |
| Notional | The amount on which swap interest is calculated; it is not exchanged |
| Hedge carry | What a swap earns or costs over a period: the floating rate received less the fixed rate paid, for a pay-fixed swap |
| Fair-value hedge | A hedge of the change in value of an asset; the swap and the hedged change are both recognised in earnings |

## 5. Limit terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Limit | A bound on a risk measure set in the Investment Policy Statement: a floor, a cap or a band |
| Utilisation | The share of a limit's allowance that is used |
| Warning | The status of a limit whose utilisation has reached the warning level but which is not breached |
| Breach | The status of a limit whose value is outside its bound |
| Headroom | The distance from a limit's value to its nearest bound, in the limit's own unit; negative when breached |
| Remediation | A change, usually a swap, that brings a breached limit back inside its bound |
| Indicative | Sized by the analytics and not approved: a proposal, not an instruction |
